Direct answer

Every numbered marginal citation on this site resolves to one of the entries below. Primary derivations are the original papers; modern references are the textbooks now used to teach the field; industry anchors back any non-math claim (carrying cost percent, 3PL pricing, food-loss percentages).

Primary derivations (Wilson EOQ, EPQ, multi-item joint)

    https://doi.org/10.1287/opre.38.6.947

    https://hbr.org/archive-toc/1934

    https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andlersche_Losgr%C3%B6%C3%9Fenformel

    https://catalog.hathitrust.org/Record/000497912

Inventory software vendor references (verified June 2026)

Where the site mentions specific ERP / inventory products as places EOQ logic shows up in practice, the pricing references are below. Sources verified 20 June 2026 from the vendor pages cited.

  • Cin7 (Cin7 Core)
    Cin7 Core Standard at $349 per month (5 users, 6,000 annual sales orders, 2 integrations, unlimited locations).
    https://www.cin7.com/pricing/
    Verified 2026-08-19
  • Zoho Inventory
    Free plan with 50 orders per month, 1 user, 2 locations. Cheapest paid plan is Standard at £25 per month (about $31) for 500 orders and 2 users.
    https://www.zoho.com/inventory/pricing/
    Verified 2026-07-25
  • Fishbowl Inventory
    Essentials at $229 per month billed annually (2 users, inventory tracking, item and SKU management, cycle counts).
    https://www.fishbowlinventory.com/pricing
    Verified 2026-08-19
  • Oracle NetSuite
    No published list price. Resellers commonly cite around $999 per month base for a small SuiteSuccess Starter footprint plus $99 per user per month, with Advanced Inventory and Demand Planning extra.
    https://www.netsuite.com/portal/products/erp/inventory-management.shtml
    Verified 2026-06-20
  • SAP S/4HANA
    No published list price; RISE with SAP S/4HANA Cloud public edition starts in the low five-figures per year for a small footprint per partner sources, scaling with FUE (Full Use Equivalent) user counts.
    https://help.sap.com/docs/SAP_S4HANA_ON-PREMISE/f899ce30af0f4ee0a4bd128cb14e1d2e/4d8a195339af412de10000000a174cb4.html
    Verified 2026-06-20
  • Odoo (Inventory + Manufacturing)
    Community edition free (self-hosted). Odoo Online starts at $0 for one app, then $24.90 per user per month (Standard) or $37.40 per user per month (Custom) for multi-app deployments.
    https://www.odoo.com/pricing-plan
    Verified 2026-08-19
