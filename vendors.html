EOQ in inventory and ERP software

EOQ is a formula, not a product. Buyers encounter Wilson’s Q* inside their inventory or ERP system as a lot-sizing rule on the item master, or as a recommendation in a planning module. Six systems cover most of the procurement market. Each handles EOQ differently; some surface Q* as a recommendation, some require the buyer to paste it into a min/max field, and at least one (SAP) gives you four named lot-sizing procedures to choose between.

The six systems we document

  • Oracle NetSuite
    Item record + Demand Planning add-on
    No published list price. Resellers commonly cite around $999 per month base for a small SuiteSuccess Starter footprint plus $99 per user per month, with Advanced Inventory and Demand Planning extra.
  • SAP S/4HANA
    MM02 material-master lot-sizing procedure EX/OP
    No published list price; RISE with SAP S/4HANA Cloud public edition starts in the low five-figures per year for a small footprint per partner sources, scaling with FUE (Full Use Equivalent) user counts.
  • Fishbowl Inventory
    Reorder point + EOQ field in part record
    Essentials at $229 per month billed annually (2 users, inventory tracking, item and SKU management, cycle counts).
  • Cin7 (Core)
    Min/Max reorder rules, EOQ external
    Cin7 Core Standard at $349 per month (5 users, 6,000 annual sales orders, 2 integrations, unlimited locations).
  • Zoho Inventory
    Reorder point, no native EOQ solver
    Free plan with 50 orders per month, 1 user, 2 locations. Cheapest paid plan is Standard at £25 per month (about $31) for 500 orders and 2 users.
  • Odoo Inventory + MRP
    Reordering rules min/max + Quantity Multiple
    Community edition free (self-hosted). Odoo Online starts at $0 for one app, then $24.90 per user per month (Standard) or $37.40 per user per month (Custom) for multi-app deployments.

What we’re not

EOQCalculator.com is not affiliated with any of these vendors. We don’t take referral fees and the pages exist to answer one buyer question per vendor: "where is the EOQ field, and how do I populate it?" Pricing notes are sourced from public pricing pages and were last verified August 2026; quote-only systems (NetSuite, SAP) list partner-disclosed ranges with the source URL.
